About Me!
Hello! My name is You Xuan I am currently studying my first year in SP!
Something interesting I like to do when I am not drowning in code and lecture notes is finding out more about sharks since they're my favourite animal.
My passion for eco-friendly living took root when I learnt about the impacts of pollution on oceans and marine life.
Ever since, I have made it a priority to always do the 5R's whenever I can! A experience I have had with waste reduction is writing a grocery list before going shopping, it allows for me to buy only what I need.
Over the years, I have picked up some green hacks I swear by!
One example is using Beeswax wraps instead of clingfilm
which reduces the amount of plastic wasted by a lot.
Another example is using bar soap,
since the packaging for it has way less surface area than a normal container of soap,
it helps to reduce plastic waste too due to the packaging!
